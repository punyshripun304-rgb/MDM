Filed under: Accounts

Click “Forgot password” on the portal login page and enter your roll number. A one-time code is sent to your registered college email.

If you can no longer access that email, visit the department office with your ID card and the password will be reset for you.

Filed under: Examinations

The timetable is posted on the department notice board and under “Examinations” on the portal at least two weeks before the exams begin.

Hall tickets are released a week before the first paper. Download and print yours, and carry it along with your college ID.

Filed under: Academics

Most courses require a minimum of 75% attendance, counted separately for theory and practical sessions. Check the exact rule in your department handbook.

If you are short because of illness or an official event, submit a request with proof to your class advisor before the exam forms close.

Filed under: Certificates

Fill in the request form at the department office and attach a photocopy of your ID card. For a no-dues certificate, your lab and library dues must be cleared first.

Bonafide certificates are normally ready within two working days.

Filed under: Labs

Slots are allotted batch-wise at the start of the semester and displayed outside each lab.

To swap, find a classmate willing to exchange and submit a signed request to the lab in-charge at least a day before the session.

Filed under: Student life

Sign-ups open in the first month of each semester. Fill the interest form on the notice board or portal, then attend the short orientation meeting.

Any year can join. No prior experience is needed.

Filed under: Placements

The training and placement cell shares all openings on the portal and by college email, so keep your profile and resume up to date.

For department-level internships, speak to your class advisor.
